SellerPrice by FutureWala

SellerPrice is an offline-ready, browser-based reverse pricing and profit calculator built for Daraz Pakistan (FBM) sellers. Enter your product purchase cost, target profit percentage, and desired discount percentage to get the exact Original List Price and Discounted Sale Price, along with a 5-step Urdu pricing guide and automatic local calculation history.

Features

Reverse Target-Margin Pricing: Automatically solves for the exact selling price needed to hit your target Cost ROI % or Selling Price Margin % after all marketplace deductions.

7,851 PK Categories (May 2026 Rates): Built-in category search autocomplete with Daraz Pakistan commission percentages.

Custom Discount Suggestion (Default 50%): Calculates the higher original list price to enter in Daraz Seller Center so your discounted sale price still protects your target profit.

Complete Fee & Tax Modeling: Includes provincial VAT (15% KPK/Sindh/Balochistan, 16% Punjab), 2.25% payment fee, Drop-off/Pickup handling fees, withholding tax (ATL & Non-ATL), packaging cost, return/RTO buffer, Daraz Ads/PPC budget, Free Shipping Max, and Voucher Max.

Detailed Urdu Seller Guide: Live, 5-part breakdown in clear Urdu with no abbreviations, plus one-click copy buttons for List Price, Sale Price, and the Urdu guide.

Local Recent History & CSV Export: Automatically saves your recent calculations in browser localStorage (no login or database required) with one-click CSV export.

How to Run Locally

Download or clone this repository.

Open index.html directly in Chrome, Edge, or any modern browser (works 100% offline).

Search your product category, enter your Purchase Cost, Target Profit (%), and Discount Off (%), then click Calculate Now.
